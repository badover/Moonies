# Moonies

### Are you ready to go to the moon?

A 2D space adventure where every decision matters. Choose your crew character, explore a three-deck spacecraft, and keep its systems running from Earth orbit to the Moon and back.

Complete randomized missions, operate onboard equipment, and protect your crew from radiation. Finish every assigned task and survive the flight to reach the final cinematic.

Choose your character: Commander · Pilot · Engineer · Medic
Flight duration: About 10 minutes
Built with: Unity
